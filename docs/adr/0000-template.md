## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and the facts a reader needs to follow
the decision. Link the issues that raised it.

## Decision drivers

- The criteria the options are judged against, most important first.

## Considered options

1. **Option one.** One-paragraph description, then its pros and cons against the drivers.
2. **Option two.** As above.

## Decision

The chosen option and why it beats the others on the drivers. State it so that a later PR can
be checked against it. If the status is `proposed`, say what evidence would decide it and who
gathers it.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer.

### Negative

- What becomes harder, what it costs, and what follow-up work it creates.

## Security impact

Required and never empty. How the decision changes the attack surface, trust boundaries,
bypass potential or failure behaviour. If the decision really has no security effect, say why.

## Links

- Issues, design docs, and ADRs this one supersedes or is superseded by.
