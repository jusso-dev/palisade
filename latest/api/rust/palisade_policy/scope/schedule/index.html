Schedules: when a scope is active (#38).

A CompiledSchedule is evaluated at an instant (seconds since the Unix epoch, from the agent’s trusted time) in one time zone, chosen by the schedule’s timezone_basis:

  • utc: Coordinated Universal Time.
  • device_local: the device’s zone (ScopeFacts::device_time_zone).
  • user_local: the session user’s zone (ScopeFacts::session_time_zone) if the agent could read it, and otherwise the device’s, with the note timezone_fallback.

Zones are IANA names, resolved with the time zone database bundled into the binary, so evaluation never reads the system.

§Windows

A window on day D from start to end is active from the instant that is start on D to the instant that is end on D (or on the next day, if end is not after start), including the first and excluding the second. 24:00 is midnight at the end of the day. A window with duration_mins lasts that many minutes of elapsed time from its start, whatever the clocks do.

Local times are turned into instants with the “compatible” rule of the ECMAScript Temporal proposal: a time that occurs twice, when clocks go back, is its first occurrence; a time that is skipped, when clocks go forward, is moved forward by the length of the gap (02:30 on a day that jumps from 02:00 to 03:00 is 03:30). So a window 01:00–03:00 on the day clocks go back lasts three hours of elapsed time, and on the day they go forward one hour.

§Dates

dates and except_dates test the local date of the instant being decided, not the day a window started on. With dates, the schedule is only active on those dates; on an except_dates date it is never active.
