Expand description

The stable code of a finding. The snake_case name is part of the API.

Variants§

§

UnsupportedSchemaVersion

The schema version’s major is not this reader’s.

§

InvalidName

A name is empty, too long, untrimmed, or contains control characters.

§

InvalidText

A free-text field is too long or contains disallowed control characters.

§

DuplicateId

Two objects share one ID.

§

UnknownRuleSet

The policy references a rule set that is not in the document.

§

DuplicateRuleSetRef

The policy lists the same rule set twice.

§

TooManyRules

The document holds more than the allowed number of rules.

§

TooManyRuleSets

The document holds or references more than the allowed number of rule sets.

§

TooManyCriteria

A rule’s match tree holds more than the allowed number of criteria.

§

TreeTooDeep

A rule’s match tree is nested too deeply.

§

EmptyGroup

An all or any node has no children.

§

InvalidValueCount

A criterion has no values, or too many.

§

InvalidValue

A criterion value is empty, too long, or not valid for its field.

§

OperatorFieldMismatch

The operator cannot be used on the field.

§

CaseSensitivityNotApplicable

case_sensitive was set on a field that has no case, such as a hash, a version or a SID.

§

SettingOutOfRange

A setting is outside its allowed range.

§

UnknownAdvancedKey

An advanced key is not in the registry of known keys.

§

InvalidAdvancedKey

An advanced key is not a well-formed identifier.

§

MandatoryNotBlocklist

mandatory is set on a rule set that is not a blocklist.

§

MandatoryRuleNotDeny

A mandatory blocklist contains a rule that does not deny.

§

MandatoryRuleNotEnforced

A rule in a mandatory blocklist is set never to be enforced.

§

InvalidDisplayId

A rule’s display_id is not R- followed by 6 to 12 digits.

§

DuplicateObjectType

An object type is listed twice in one rule.

§

InvalidTag

Too many tags, or a tag that is empty, too long, or repeated.

§

WeakHashOnlyAllow

The only identity of a rule that lets an object run, trusts it or excludes it is SHA-1 or MD5 (ADR 0008, #34).

§

AllowWinsNotRecommended

The allow_wins model is not recommended for Essential Eight (ADR 0004).

§

NoIdentityAnchor

A path through a rule’s tree has no positive identity criterion: a path of negations or context alone in a rule that lets an object run, trusts it or excludes it (#34), or any rule’s path that includes a context criterion (#33). The message names the nodes on one such path.

§

NegationOnly

Every path through a deny or audit rule’s tree that has no positive identity criterion is made of negations alone, which match almost every object (#32).

§

UnsupportedRegex

A regular expression uses look-around or a backreference, which the linear-time engine does not support (#32).

§

InvalidRegex

A regular expression is not valid.

§

PatternTooLarge

A regular expression or wildcard compiles to more than the size limit (#32).

§

TooManyRegexes

The policy holds more regular expressions than allowed (#32).

§

UnindexedRules

More enabled rules than ALWAYS_EVAL_WARN have no index anchor, so they are evaluated for every object of their types (#42). A warning.

§

WeakIdentity

The strongest guaranteed identity of a rule that lets an object run, trusts it or excludes it is weak, such as a file name or version-resource string (#31, #34).

§

ActionNotValidInRuleSet

The action is not valid in a rule set of this kind (#34).

§

ActionNotValidForObjectType

The action, or one of its options, is not valid for an object type the rule applies to (#34).

§

InvalidActionOption

An action option is malformed or inconsistent (#34).

§

LogLevelNotAllowed

log: never or log: summary on an action that needs an event per decision (#34).

§

InvalidNotification

A notification text, notification setting or help URL is not valid (#34).

§

InvalidEffect

An effect flag is outside its allowed range (#34).

§

EffectsNotApplicable

effects on an action that makes no decision (#34).

§

ExclusionNeedsPermission

An ignore rule: a performance exclusion that needs the admin.exclusions permission at the server (#34).

§

OrderRequired

Under first_match, a rule that allows or denies has no order (#35).

§

OrderReserved

Under first_match, a rule of a custom rule set has an order in a reserved range (#35, order_ranges).

§

OrderOutOfRange

A rule’s order is above 1000000 (#35).

§

PriorityRequired

Under priority, a rule that allows or denies has no priority (#35).

§

DuplicatePriority

Under priority, two rules with different actions share a priority, so the fixed action order decides between them (#35).

§

PrecedenceOptionUnused

A precedence option is set that the selected model does not use (#35).

§

ModeNotAllowed

A mode is set where it is not allowed: disabled for a policy, an object type or a rule set (#36).

§

ModeDisconnectedWeaker

mode_disconnected is neither the policy’s mode nor stricter, so losing the server would relax the device, or it would never apply (#36).

§

ModeOverrideNotAllowed

mode_override on a rule set that is not a blocklist, or on a mandatory blocklist (#36, ADR 0004).

§

EnforcementOverrideNotDeny

enforcement_override other than inherit on a rule that does not deny: only a block can be enforced or relaxed (#36).

§

UnresolvedRef

A match tree still holds a library reference (ref). References are replaced with their trees when the effective policy is built (#37).

§

InvalidCategory

A category_path is not Category or Category/Subcategory with valid parts (#37).

§

SuppressNotAllowed

allow_child_suppress: true on a mandatory rule set, whose rules can never be suppressed (#37, ADR 0004).

§

InvalidScope

A scope is malformed: a value not valid for its kind, a list over its limit, an unknown location profile, or an option on rules it does not apply to (#38).

§

InvalidSchedule

A schedule is malformed: a window, a date range or a window-close option (#38).

§

UserPatternNotAllowed

user_pattern in a position where a spoofed account name would make a rule that lets an object run apply, or keep a deny from applying (#38).

§

OptionNotApplicable

A criterion option is set where it does not apply, such as anchored on an operator that takes no regular expression (#32), or safety_critical outside a mandatory blocklist (#32, #87).

§

UnknownLevelNotValidForType

An unknown-handling level that cannot apply to the object type it is set for: restrict other than for executables, scripts and installers, or prompt for libraries and drivers (#39).

§

RestrictProfileRequired

A restrict level with no restrict_profile (#39).

§

InvalidPromptSettings

The prompt settings are inconsistent: no options, a repeated option, or a prompt fallback (#39).

§

UnknownAllow

An unknown-handling level is allow: unknown objects run silently (#39).

§

UnknownAllowNotPermitted

A policy tagged with an Essential Eight preset lets unknown objects in user profiles or temporary folders run silently (#39, ISM-1870).

§

ComplianceWeakened

A policy tagged with a compliance preset is weaker than the preset for some object, or lacks what the preset requires (#39, #17).

§

DenyMatchesSelf

A deny would match one of Palisade’s own pinned images (#39).

§

MandatoryBlocklistRequired

The e8_ml3 preset needs a mandatory blocklist, and the policy references none (#39).

§

SignatureStatusRelaxed

A signer criterion matches signatures that the default would not trust: allow_digest_mismatch, or an accept_status beyond the default in a rule that lets an object run, trusts it or excludes it (#31).

§

InvalidActivityWindow

expires_at is not after activates_at (#40).

§

InvalidGovernance

A governance setting is out of range or repeated, or lowers a finding’s severity below its default (#40).

§

JustificationRequired

In governance mode, a rule that needs a justification has none (#40).

§

ReviewDateRequired

In governance mode, an allow or trust rule has no review date and no creation time to derive one from (#40).

§

ExceptionIncomplete

In governance mode, an exception rule has no expiry, no approved_at, an expiry more than 365 days after approved_at, no approver, or no risk rating (#40).

§

OnExpireNotApplicable

on_expire on a rule without expires_at, or convert_to_audit on a rule of a mandatory rule set (#40).

§

ReservedRuleSetId

A policy document holds a rule set with the managed system_baseline’s well-known ID, which only the engine adds, after validation (#39, #40).
