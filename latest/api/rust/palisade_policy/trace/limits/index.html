The limits that bound a trace.

Constants§

MAX_DECISION_LIST
The most entries of each list of the decision, in the trace’s JSON.
MAX_FAILING
The most failing criteria listed per near miss.
MAX_INACTIVE
The most disabled rules that would have matched, listed.
MAX_INHERITANCE
The most steps of the deciding rule’s provenance: the hierarchy nodes it came from (#37) and the policy, rule set and rule. A hierarchy is at most 8 groups deep, so 32 steps hold every layer and its template.
MAX_MATCHED
The most matched rules listed.
MAX_NEAR_MISSES
The most near misses listed (#41: “capped at 10”).
MAX_RULES_PER_ENTRY
The most rules named by one unknown fact or one warning.
MAX_SCOPE_REJECTIONS
The most scope rejections listed.
MAX_TRACE_BYTES
The most bytes of a trace’s JSON (compact form), for the largest trace the other limits allow.
