Expand description

Something unusual about one signature that does not by itself change its status.

Variants (Non-exhaustive)§

This enum is marked as non-exhaustive
Non-exhaustive enums could have additional variants added in future. Therefore, when matching against variants of non-exhaustive enums, an extra wildcard arm must be added to account for any future variants.
§

TrailingData

Bytes follow the SignedData inside its WIN_CERTIFICATE entry. A few zero bytes of padding are normal and not reported; anything else is unsigned data hidden in a signed file.

Fields

§len: usize

How many bytes.

§all_zero: bool

Whether they are all zero.

§

Sha1Digest

The image or signer digest is SHA-1, which is not collision resistant. The signature loses primary selection to any SHA-2 one.

§

SmallRsaKey(u32)

The signer’s RSA key is smaller than 2048 bits. It still verifies, for old files, but ADR 0008 sets 2048 bits as the floor for trust.

§

DigestAlgorithmsDiffer

The SignerInfo’s digest algorithm differs from the indirect data’s. Each is checked with its own algorithm.

§

SignerDigestNotListed

The SignedData’s digestAlgorithms set does not list the signer’s digest algorithm.

§

SignerVersionMismatch(u32)

The SignerInfo version does not match its signer identifier form (1 for issuer and serial, 3 for a key identifier).

§

AmbiguousSignerCertificate

More than one certificate matches the signer identifier; the first is used.

§

UnparseableCertificate(usize)

A certificate in the blob is valid DER but not a certificate this crate can read (an unexpected structure, or SPKI parameters that are not allowed for its key). It is kept in SignatureInfo::certs_in_blob but cannot be the signer. Invalid DER in any certificate makes the whole signature SignatureStatus::Malformed instead.

§

TooManyCertificates

The blob holds more than super::MAX_CERTIFICATES certificates; the rest were ignored.

§

NonX509Certificate

A CertificateChoices entry that is not an X.509 certificate was skipped.

§

UnknownAuthenticatedAttribute(ObjectId)

An authenticated attribute of an unknown type was ignored.

§

UnknownUnauthenticatedAttribute(ObjectId)

An unauthenticated attribute of an unknown type was ignored.

§

TextTruncated

A text field was longer than 4096 characters and was cut.

§

UnreadLinkForm

A SpcString or SpcLink used a form this crate does not read (a moniker or file link as moreInfo, for example).

§

PageHashesMalformed

The page hash moniker’s table is not a whole number of records, or its structure is not the expected one. It was dropped.

§

NestingTooDeep

A nested signature was not parsed because it is deeper than super::MAX_NESTING_DEPTH.

§

LimitReached

A nested signature or timestamp was not parsed because a limit was reached (super::MAX_SIGNATURES signatures, or super::MAX_TIMESTAMPS timestamps per signature).

§

WarningsTruncated

More than super::MAX_WARNINGS warnings; the rest were dropped.

§

SignedMetadataUnreadable(ObjectId)

A signed metadata attribute (1.3.6.1.4.1.311.2.7.1 to .10) whose value is not a string, does not decode exactly, or is longer than 4096 characters. It is left out of SignedMetadata, so no rule can match a partial or replaced value.
