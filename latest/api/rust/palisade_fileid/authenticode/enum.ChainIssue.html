Expand description

Something found while building a chain. Certificates are indices into ChainReport::certificates.

UnhandledCriticalExtension

A certificate has a critical extension this crate does not process, so it cannot be part of a chain (RFC 5280, 4.2).

Fields

§cert: usize

The certificate.

§oid: ObjectId

The extension.

IssuerNotCa

A certificate with the right name is not a CA (no basicConstraints with cA set), so it cannot be an issuer.

Fields

§issuer: usize

The would-be issuer.

§issuer: usize

SignatureInvalid

A certificate’s signature does not verify with the key of a certificate whose name matches its issuer: a forged or unrelated issuer.

Fields

§child: usize

The child.

§issuer: usize

The would-be issuer.

SignatureUnsupported

A certificate’s signature could not be checked with a would-be issuer’s key.

Fields

§child: usize

The child.

§issuer: usize

The would-be issuer.

§why: Unsupported

Why.

§

Loop

A path would revisit a certificate.

Fields

§cert: usize

The certificate that would be revisited.

§

TooLong

A path would be longer than MAX_CHAIN_LENGTH.

§

TooManyPaths

More than MAX_CHAIN_PATHS paths exist; the rest were not built.

§

TooManyCertificates

More than MAX_EMBEDDED_CERTIFICATES embedded certificates; the rest were ignored.

§

WorkLimitReached

The signature-check budget (MAX_SIGNATURE_CHECKS per build, MAX_FILE_SIGNATURE_CHECKS per file) ran out; further links were not made, and further self-issued certificates were not treated as self-signed.

§

IssuesTruncated

More than MAX_CHAIN_ISSUES per-certificate issues; the rest were dropped.
