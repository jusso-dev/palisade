What is wrong with a rule.

Variants (Non-exhaustive)§

This enum is marked as non-exhaustive
Non-exhaustive enums could have additional variants added in future. Therefore, when matching against variants of non-exhaustive enums, an extra wildcard arm must be added to account for any future variants.
§

TooManyRules

More than MAX_RULES rules, or more than MAX_STRINGS strings over all rules.

§

NoStrings

A rule has no strings.

§

TooManyStrings

A rule has more than MAX_STRINGS_PER_RULE strings.

§

BadTag

The tag is not two to four [a-z0-9-] segments of 1 to 32 bytes joined by :, or is longer than MAX_TAG_LEN.

§

ReservedTag

The tag is a built-in tag; the built-in set is read-only.

§

DuplicateTag

Two rules have the same tag.

§

BadHex

Hex text that is not pairs of hex digits or ?.

§

EmptyPattern

A pattern of no bytes.

§

PatternTooLong

A pattern longer than MAX_PATTERN_LEN bytes.

§

WideNotAscii

Wide text that is not ASCII.

§

NoAtom

A string that is not at a fixed offset has no run of MIN_ATOM_LEN exact bytes.

§

BadCondition

Condition::AtLeast of zero or of more than the strings.

§

BadLocation

An empty Location::Range, or a Location::Tail of zero or more than TAIL_SCAN_LEN.

§

TooLarge

The search automaton could not be built.
