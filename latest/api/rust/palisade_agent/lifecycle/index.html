The agent’s operating states and the legal transitions between them.

FromTo
StartingRunning, Degraded, SafeMode, FailSafe, Stopping
RunningDegraded, Updating, FailSafe, Stopping
DegradedRunning, Degraded (new reasons), Updating, FailSafe, Stopping
UpdatingRunning, Degraded, FailSafe, Stopping
SafeModeFailSafe, Stopping
FailSafeFailSafe (new reason), Stopping
StoppingStopped
Stoppednothing

FailSafe is left only by stopping: a critical subsystem that reached it is not restarted again until the service restarts. What enforcement does in that state is governed by FailMode. Safe mode lasts for the whole boot. The supervisor drives Starting, Running, Degraded, FailSafe, Stopping and Stopped. SafeMode (safe-boot detection) and Updating (#66) are entered by the components that own them when they land.
